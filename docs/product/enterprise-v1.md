# Sloppy Enterprise v1 launch brief

This is an internal commercial plan, not public pricing or a binding offer.

## Initial customer

The first customer profile is an engineering or platform team running Sloppy
as an internal control plane for coding and operational agents.

Enterprise v1 is limited to generic OIDC, group-to-role mapping, five
enterprise roles, project/tool policy, centralized approval rules, redacted
append-only audit with JSONL export/retention, and an offline Ed25519
entitlement. SAML, SCIM, SIEM streaming, formal SLA, HA certification, and
managed cloud are explicitly deferred.

## Discovery gates

Do not complete the private Enterprise implementation before:

- 10 customer interviews are documented;
- 3 design partners provide written commitments; and
- at least 1 customer pays for a scoped deployment.

GA additionally requires one production design partner, a successful OIDC
deployment, a verified audit export, and a documented renewal/update workflow.

## Internal commercial anchors

- Design Partner: USD 5k–10k/year, up to 10 human users, one production
  control plane, onboarding included.
- Business: target ACV USD 12k–25k/year.
- Enterprise/OEM: from USD 25k/year with separately negotiated scope.
- Implementation/security review: USD 3k–10k.
- Premium support: separate Order Form line item.

Public surfaces say **Contact sales** and do not publish a fixed price. Order
Forms price active human users and a production control plane; they do not
meter agents, tasks, nodes, tools, tokens, or model usage.

Redistribution, OEM embedding, and providing Sloppy to third parties as a
service require separate agreements. Managed Cloud, marketplace revenue
share, and OEM programs wait until demand for the core Enterprise SKU is
demonstrated.
